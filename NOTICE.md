# Notice

design-taste is released under the MIT License (see LICENSE).

design-taste is Anthony Tackett's design prescription. Its core rules (`references/principles/*`, `evidence.md`) come from his own comparison rounds.

Rules tagged `default` in the reference files are unvalidated by Anthony's rounds until promoted. Validated rules always win.

## Field review credits

In October 2026 the rules were compared with other open design-guidance projects. Ideas were adapted and reworded; no text was copied. Projects reviewed, and what they prompted: pbakaus/impeccable (Apache-2.0: deterministic detector rules, product-versus-visual context, craft floor); MarioCasanovacf/justesse (MIT: evaluation harness, declaration gate); funboy322/avoid-ai-design (MIT: second-order defaults, detectability tags); aievolutionpl/agent-design-taste (MIT: veto gates, blockers override scores, observable rules); educlopez/ui-craft (MIT: deterministic score, description testing); sshahzaiib/senior-designer-skill (MIT: platform gate, assertion evals, sourced evidence); deeflect/mies (facets and anti-reference intake); DonkeyKing01/tasteful-ui-skill (reject a harmful reference); anthropics/skills frontend-design (plan-then-review pass; the tells list); Leonxlnx/taste-skill, miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop, emilkowalski/skill, LottieFiles/motion-design-skill, LeoStehlik/no-slop-ui (MIT: motion claimed and shown, filter-versus-direction, motion gate, activation boundary); emilkowalski/skills (MIT: worst-case content stress testing, interruptible and asymmetric interface motion, gesture-release feel, the phone platform layer, cause-first frame diagnosis, the motion opportunity pass with reported rejections, divergent variants; Apple fluid-interface principles as that project distills them). In that review the rules were adapted as reasons, not values: his fixed curves, durations, scale amounts and spring settings were deliberately left out.
