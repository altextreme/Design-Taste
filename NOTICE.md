# Notice

design-taste is Anthony Tackett's design prescription. Its core rules (`references/principles/*`, `evidence.md`) come from his own comparison rounds.

The imported reference files (`ui-craft.md`, `process-and-critique.md`, `formats.md`, `media-prompts.md`, `scripts-and-direction.md`, `brand-and-registers.md`, `open-design-crosswalk.md`) were derived from an analysis of the open-source Open Design project (<https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design>, Apache License 2.0). All text here is paraphrased and reorganized to fit this skill's layer model; nothing is copied wholesale. Open Design's `craft/` notes are themselves adapted from the MIT-licensed refero_skill, and some of its prompt templates are CC-BY-4.0. Credit and thanks to the Open Design contributors.

Imported rules are marked `OD` and are unvalidated by Anthony's rounds until promoted. Validated rules always win.
