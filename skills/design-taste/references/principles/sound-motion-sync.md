# Design Taste: Sound / Motion Sync

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

- **With music, transitions snap to the beat.** When a piece has a music bed, each transition waits for the next beat. That's enough to feel locked to the music without the long waits of phrase-level syncing. Snapping only ever extends holds, never shortens them, so the reading-time rule still governs. Without music, timing follows reading time alone, at a steady tempo. `L1` · R052, R053
- **With a voiceover, the voice drives the edit.** Each frame arrives as its line begins, so what is seen and heard land together. Letting the visuals lead by a beat is an acceptable alternative. Visuals running on their own schedule, unsynced to the voice, is not. The reading-time floor still applies: a frame never leaves before its text can be read, even if the line ends sooner. `L1` · R055
- **No sound effects by default.** The music bed carries the audio. Whooshes on transitions and ticks on entrances add nothing to a piece like this. Reserve sound effects for a brief whose tone genuinely calls for them. `L1` default · R054 (energetic or playful presets untested)
