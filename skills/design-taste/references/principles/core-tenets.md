# Design Taste: Core Tenets

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

These recur across many rounds and override stylistic defaults:
0. **Concept drives execution. Always. But the design principles still hold.** The subject and its imagery choose the voice: typeface, palette temperature, motion energy, crop, camera. The foundation rules never bend: hierarchy, reading order, contrast, reading time, intentionality and restraint in structure. Every L2 preset below is a consequence of this tenet, not a style to apply by default. (R011, R020, R051, R056–R061)
1. **Everything must read as intentional.** Every placement, deviation, color and motion choice must be visibly deliberate. Anything that could be mistaken for an accident or a default is wrong, even if it's technically fine. (R001, R008, R013, R022, R050)
2. **Hierarchy is enforced, and it follows meaning.** The reader never wonders what comes first. Rank, weight, color and motion order all follow the content's meaning and reading order. (R001–R003, R014, R027, R034, R043)
3. **Restraint over effect.** Moderate steps, no gratuitous bold, no bounce, no shadows, no lone accents. Drama is concentrated in one place, the headline or image, and everything else stays calm. (R024, R027, R031, R041, R048, R015)
4. **Legibility is non-negotiable.** Accessible contrast, enough reading time in motion, no glyph collisions, no near-tangents. (R009, R020, R029, R046)
5. **Decide by the concept and the real assets.** Crops, camera moves, tints and dark modes are chosen for what this subject and this photograph need. (R020, R034, R038, R047)
