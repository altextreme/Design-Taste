# Design Taste: Tone & Emotional Register

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

- **Register must match the subject's meaning.** Tone is read off the content first. A loud, heavy, condensed treatment applied to a quiet, contemplative subject doesn't read as bold. It reads as comical. A mismatch between voice and subject is a failure regardless of how well-crafted each part is. `L1` · R011
- **Tone moves the dials, never the skeleton.** Across registers the structure holds: the layout, reading order, image-first logic, hierarchy, accessible contrast and one text block. Tone changes the voice: the typeface, palette brightness and size, and decorative containers. A quiet subject gets an editorial serif with muted, restrained color. A playful subject gets a friendly rounded sans, brighter saturated color, more hues, and color fields behind labels and key details. Applying the quiet house style unchanged to a playful brief is wrong. `L1` (structure) · `L2` (voice) · R056
- **Playful, but disciplined, and pushed with confidence.** For playful work, go further than a timid recolor: solid color pills and badges behind labels and dates are welcome, as is a broader palette. Everything stays square to the grid, with no sticker tilts, and the headline stays one color. Playful energy comes from type, color and containers, never from breaking alignment or splitting an element's color. `L2 · playful` · R056, R057
- **Luxury: a deep jewel-toned field, a high-contrast Didone at full display scale, and metallic warmth.** A dark field drawn from the imagery (velvet plum, for example) reads as premium. A high-contrast Didone headline at a large size, white on the field, carries the luxury voice best, ahead of light tracked caps. A gold accent marks the date. The headline keeps its dominant scale. Luxury restraint applies to ornament and palette, never to hierarchy. `L2 · luxury` · R059, R060
- **No fixed default register: tone is read from the subject every time.** Across quiet, playful, energetic and luxury briefs, the choice always followed the content. Asked twice for a personal default, no answer was given, and the behavior shows why: the subject sets the register. `L1` · R011, R056–R060
