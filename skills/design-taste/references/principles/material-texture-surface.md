# Design Taste: Material / Texture / Surface

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

- **Clean by default. When texture is used, use fine grain or noise over the whole piece.** Clean, flat surfaces are the default. When a piece calls for texture, the preferred treatment is a fine, unified grain or noise across everything, image and type together, as one coherent medium. Texture is a per-piece, concept-driven decision. `L1` default · R062
- **Gradients: welcome when careful, never overdone.** A subtle tonal gradient within a single hue, such as a field deepening away from the image, adds elegance and is the default gradient move. Two-hue gradients are acceptable, and can look very good, when the concept or the actual colors in the imagery suggest both hues. A gradient should be felt more than noticed. `L1` (subtle tonal) · `L2/L3` (multi-hue, imagery-driven) · R063
- **No simulated materials.** Faked paper tooth, fiber or other skeuomorphic substrate effects applied as a background are rejected. Texture should read as a property of the medium, like grain or noise, not an imitation of a physical material. `L1` · R062
- **Flat and simple is the elegant default.** Type and surfaces stay flat. Glows, drop shadows, bevels, emboss and extrusion read as gimmicky, and bevel-and-emboss with drop shadow reads as dated. `L1` · R015, R064
- **Effects are allowed for one of two reasons (plus interactive elevation, see Interaction).**
  - **Function:** a shadow or glow that is necessary for legibility, such as separating type from a busy image, or a panel over a full-page image.
  - **Concept in motion:** an effect that embodies the idea dynamically, such as a glow that blooms as part of an animation, or a shadow that shifts direction as a light source moves across the headline. Static decorative effects fail this test.

  Any effect must align with the concept and be executed with restraint and elegance. `L1` · R015, R051, R064
- **Texture and effects must carry meaning.** Surface treatments, whether grain, glow or dimension, are justified by the concept or the medium, never added as decoration. `L1` · R062, R064
