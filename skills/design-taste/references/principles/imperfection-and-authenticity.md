# Design Taste: Imperfection & Authenticity

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

- **Precise and digital by default. Imperfection only when the concept asks for it.** Hand-made marks and glitch or error aesthetics are legitimate tools for the right creative piece, but they must be read from the room. For refined, institutional or quiet subjects they don't belong. `L1` default · `L2` (concept-driven) · R081
