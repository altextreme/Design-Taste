# Design Taste: Brand Flexibility

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

- **Respect the brand typeface over personal taste.** When guidelines mandate a typeface, use it. Don't swap in a preferred face, even for the headline. Apply the house principles inside the brand's type, such as hierarchy, weight emphasis, spacing and measure. `L3` · R087
- **When a brand rule breaks a non-negotiable, adjust minimally and flag it.** Example: a brand color that fails contrast on text. Keep the brand's intent, and change only what the non-negotiable requires, such as darkening the color only where text needs contrast. Tell the client about the conflict explicitly. Never ship the failing version silently, and never use the conflict as a pretext to override other brand rules. `L3` · R009, R087
- **The non-negotiables that survive any brand override:**
  1. Accessible text contrast.
  2. Every frame holds long enough to read all of its text.
  3. Ordinary text links inside paragraphs are colored and underlined at rest; navigation, linked wordmarks, and button-styled links use their own cues (R155).
  4. No glyph collisions and no near-tangents.
  5. Reading order and enforced hierarchy.

  *(Proposed in R087 and not contested.)*
- **Brand guidelines override personal color and image-treatment preferences.** If a brand document fixes the palette or prescribes a photographic look, it wins over taste-driven choices such as sampling colors from the image or grading naturally. Only the non-negotiables survive the override: accessible contrast and reading time. `L3` · R061, R066
- **Non-negotiable: in motion, hold every frame long enough to read all of its text.** R046
- **Non-negotiable: accessible text contrast.** If a brand palette would produce text that fails contrast standards, flag it and fix the pairing. Don't comply silently. `L1` · R009 (partial — other non-negotiables still to probe)
