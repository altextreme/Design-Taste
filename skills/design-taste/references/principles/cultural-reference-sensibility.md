# Design Taste: Cultural / Reference Sensibility

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

- **Avoid effects that date a piece.** Treatments strongly tied to a past era of digital design, like bevel-and-emboss and heavy drop shadows, are avoided. `L1` · R064
- **Sensibility is a concept choice. Be fluent in all of them.** Timeless and original, trend-forward, and nostalgic or reference-heavy are all valid registers, chosen by the concept. Trend-forward editorial work (oversized tight grotesks, mono labels, exposed grids, acid accents) is admired as cool and modern. The agent must be able to execute each sensibility convincingly, with the foundation rules intact. Being dated by accident is the failure; choosing a period deliberately is fine. `L2` · R064, R080
