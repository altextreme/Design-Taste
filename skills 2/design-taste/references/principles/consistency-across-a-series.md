# Design Taste: Consistency Across a Series

> Layer tags: `L1` foundation (always), `L2` tonal preset (only for that tone), `L3` brand-negotiable. `Rnnn` = the comparison round that produced the rule (see ../evidence.md). Non-negotiables in SKILL.md survive any brand override.

- **Vary the layout. Keep the brand language constant.** A series should show real variety from piece to piece, with different layouts suited to each image, while clearly reading as one brand. The type system, color logic, hierarchy, label system and voice hold steady. Variety in structure, consistency in language. `L1` · R078
- **Variation must be decisive.** If pieces are nearly identical in layout, small per-piece swaps, such as different headline colors, read as inconsistency rather than intent. Either hold a template strictly or vary meaningfully. This is the series form of "deviate decisively." `L1` · R013, R078
- **A recurring signature element strengthens a series.** One element that repeats across every piece, like a large exhibition numeral, adds design and branding without fighting the variety. It must be meaningful (a real number, or a real logo). A typeset name standing in for a logo is weak; use the actual logo if one exists. Align the signature to the grid, for example flush with the text's left edge, or set it over the photograph. It should never float in a leftover spot. `L1` · R079
- **Never shrink the image to a token.** Even in a type-led variant, a small inset thumbnail photo is a weak treatment. The image keeps a substantial, intentional presence. `L1` · R078
